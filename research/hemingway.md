# 海明威寫作法研究摘要（voice profile 的 evidence base）

> 目的：把「海明威寫作法」拆成可查證的來源層，對照 sepia 已有的量測軸，決定哪些部分能進 sepia、以什麼身分進。來源 metadata 見 [sources.md](sources.md)。本文引文皆為英文原文逐字，未翻譯改寫；受著作權保護的來源每段連續引文不超過 50 字（以空白切分計數）；中文為摘要與推論，標「Sepia 推論」者非來源所述。

## 一句話結論

海明威方法有三層，證據等級不同：（1）他本人對方法的陳述（冰山／省略、真句、寫作紀律），屬作者自述；（2）堪薩斯市星報 1915 年風格單，屬新聞寫作規範，他自己說是「the best rules I ever learned in the business of writing」；（3）後人的量化與批評研究（Levin 1951、Smith 1983、Baker 1972、Lamb 2010、Rice 2017、Ihrmark & Nilsson 2021、Lian et al. 2025）。量化研究打破兩個流行說法：他的副詞比一般文本多（但是 then／now／never 這類時間地點副詞，不是 -ly 方式副詞），而且短句對他的定義隨生涯遞減。對 sepia 而言，冰山／省略直接對上 StoryScope 量到的「主題過度決定」軸，是反 AI 味最有力的一條；但它是一種強烈美學，只能以 voice profile 身分進來，不能寫進校準核心。

## 來源層一：海明威本人的陳述

| 來源 | 逐字引文 | 方法要點 |
|---|---|---|
| *Death in the Afternoon*（1932，ch. 16） | 兩段分別引用："If a writer of prose knows enough of what he is writing about he may omit things that he knows and the reader, if the writer is writing truly enough, will have a feeling of those things as strongly as though the writer had stated them."（46 字）；"The dignity of movement of an ice-berg is due to only one-eighth of it being above water."（17 字） | 省略的前提是**知道**。省略不等於不知道或含糊。 |
| Paris Review 訪談（1958，George Plimpton） | "I always try to write on the principle of the iceberg. There is seven-eighths of it underwater for every part that shows. Anything you know you can eliminate and it only strengthens your iceberg. It is the part that doesn't show." | 同上；「eliminate」是動作，對象是「you know」的東西。 |
| "The Art of the Short Story"（1959 稿，身後刊出） | "You could omit anything if you knew that you omitted and the omitted part would strengthen the story and make people feel something more than they understood." | 省略的效果目標：讓讀者「feel more than they understood」。 |
| *A Moveable Feast*（1964） | "If I started to write elaborately, or like someone introducing or presenting something, I found that I could cut that scrollwork or ornament out and throw it away and start with the first true simple declarative sentence I had written." | 刪的是「introducing or presenting」的姿態（導引句）與裝飾；留的是簡單直述句。同章另有「write one true sentence」一語，此處不重引。 |
| "Monologue to the Maestro"（*Esquire*, 1935-10） | "Good writing is true writing." / "After you learn to write your whole object is to convey everything, every sensation, sight, feeling, place and emotion to the reader." / "It is made; not described." / "The best way is always to stop when you are going good and when you know what will happen next." / "The best way is to read it all every day from the start, correcting as you go along, then go on from where you stopped the day before." | 目標是傳達感官與情緒，手段是「made, not described」；另有兩條工作紀律（停在順手處、每天從頭重讀）。 |

## 來源層二：堪薩斯市星報風格單（1915，公有領域）

海明威 1917 至 1918 年在該報任職。風格單中他日後最常被引用的規則：

| 規則（逐字） | 類別 |
|---|---|
| "Use short sentences. Use short first paragraphs. Use vigorous English. Be positive, not negative." | 句法、段落、語氣 |
| "Avoid the use of adjectives, especially such extravagant ones as splendid, gorgeous, grand, magnificent, etc." | 詞彙 |
| "Never use old slang. … Slang to be enjoyable must be fresh." | 詞彙 |
| "Eliminate every superfluous word." | 密度 |
| "She was born in Ireland and came to Jackson County in 1874," not "but came to Jackson County." … "This is common abuse of the conjunction." | 邏輯連接詞不得偷渡因果或對比 |
| "He died of heart disease, not heart failure — everybody dies of heart failure." | 精確 |

這一層是**專業文體**的規範，不是小說技法。對 sepia 的意義是：海明威方法天然分成兩半，冰山對小說路線，星報規則對專業路線，正好對上 sepia 的兩條路由。

## 來源層三：研究

| 來源 | 類型 | 與本題相關的發現 |
|---|---|---|
| Harry Levin, "Observations on the Style of Ernest Hemingway," *Kenyon Review* XIII(4), 1951, pp. 581–609 | 文學批評（經典） | 最早系統分析其句法的論文：以並列（parataxis）與 "and" 串接取代從屬結構；名詞與動詞承重、形容詞退位。本摘要僅引書目，正文未取得。 |
| Carlos Baker, *Hemingway: The Writer as Artist*, 1972 | 文學批評 | 其技法是 "how to get the most from the least, how to prune language and avoid waste motion"；敘事複雜度 "operate under the surface"。 |
| Paul Smith, "Hemingway's Early Manuscripts: The Theory and Practice of Omission," *Journal of Modern Literature* 10(2), 1983, pp. 268–288 | 手稿研究 | 以 "Out of Season"（刪去老人自縊的結局）與 "Big Two-Hearted River"（刪去長段關於寫作的內心獨白）手稿證明省略是刻意的修訂動作，目的是提高經驗強度而非簡化。 |
| Robert Paul Lamb, *Art Matters: Hemingway, Craft, and the Creation of the Modern Short Story*, LSU Press, 2010 | 專著 | 分析作者隱退（學自 Maupassant、Chekhov）、省略、開頭與結尾結構，以及他為小說對話創造的「前所未有的角色」。 |
| Justin Rice, "What Makes Hemingway Hemingway?" LitCharts Analitics, 2017（以 Brown Corpus 為基準） | 資料新聞（非同儕審查） | 副詞比平均**多**，但幾乎都是 then／now／never 這類短的時間地點頻率副詞，"-ly" 方式副詞極少；對話量是一般作家的兩倍；短句對他的定義隨生涯遞減；晚期作品「talk more but say less」。 |
| Daniel Ihrmark & Johan Nilsson, "A Corpus Stylistic Analysis of Development in Hemingway's Literary Production," *The Hemingway Review* 40(2), 2021, pp. 71–93 | 語料庫文體學 | 以詞類分布、句長、詞彙密度做歷時分析，檢驗既有關於其風格的說法。全文未取得，僅引摘要。 |
| Jianfeng Lian et al., "The Corpus-based Multidimensional Analysis of Hemingway's *The Torrents of Spring*," *World Journal of English Language* 15(5), 2025, doi:10.5430/wjel.v15n5p232 | 語料庫（Biber MD） | 21,709 tokens 的首作對照 725,674 tokens 的後八部：首作名詞更多、詞更長、介系詞更多、因果從屬更少；後期「more interactive and emotionally nuanced」。 |
| Hemingway Editor（hemingwayapp.com，工具） | 工具規則 | 標記副詞、被動、限定詞（"maybe", "I think"）、有更簡單同義詞的字、難讀句；預設目標九年級閱讀等級；宣稱海明威的成人小說落在五年級。 |

兩點提醒。第一，Hemingway Editor 那類規則全在字句層。StoryScope 在所研究的 LAMP 編輯條件與樣本中量到，字句風格改寫後偵測率只從 95.5% 降到 93.9%；StoryScope 沒有測試「只用字句層工具」這個介入，但這個量測結果就是 sepia 不以純字句層工具為核心的理由。它是 sepia 要區別的那一類 humanizer，不是 sepia 要吸收的東西。第二，Rice 的副詞發現直接對上 style-pass §4（Reinhart：instruct-tuned 模型壓抑 discourse particles 與 plain connectives）：海明威式的 then／now／and 正是被壓抑的人類語域。

## 對照 sepia 已量測的軸（Sepia 推論）

| 海明威手法 | StoryScope／LAMP 量到的軸 | 方向 | 風險 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 冰山／省略：不講主題、不講結局的意義 | Thematic explicitness 3.28 vs 3.94；Narratorial thematic commentary 52% vs 77%；Reference explicitness | 推向人類帶，是最直接的反制 | 省略的前提是作者知道被省略的事。模型「省略」自己不知道的東西只會產出含糊；必須配 sepia 的 never-invent-specifics 與 specificity 檢查 |
| 情緒用行為與對話呈現，少用身體感受與命名 | Emotion via embodied sensation 38% vs 81%；via explicit labels 29% vs 8%；Depth of interior access 3.67 vs 3.93 | 行為主導落在人類帶（與 NCW 實驗同向） | 把 interior access 推到低極端會觸發 rubric 的 over-correction advisory；海明威自己也用直白命名（"he felt quite sure that he would never die"） |
| 對話量兩倍於平均；資訊靠人說出來 | Protagonist introduced in-dialogue（人類最強單一標記）；Gemini 指紋 "Direct speech dominates exchanges" | 雙向：人類標記，也是一個 AI 家族的指紋 | 對話比例是校準參數，不是越多越好 |
| 短直述句 ＋ 偶發的 "and" 並列長句 | Reinhart：句長變異是語體參數；voice-skills：均勻節奏是硬 finding | 短句本身不是人味，**長短交錯**才是 | 全篇短句＝節拍器，正是 voice-skills 已記錄的 house-style 指紋；Rice 的數據顯示海明威自己並非全短句 |
| 星報「Be positive, not negative」 | style-pass §4：合成文本的否定句只有人類一半，建議補回否定 | **衝突** | voice 與 sepia 校準直接相反，依 voice-skills 優先序：攤給使用者，不靜默覆蓋 |
| 星報「Avoid adjectives」、刪 superfluous words | LAMP 七類 artifact 之 redundant exposition、purple prose；style-pass §3 inflation adjectives | 同向 | 這一層是 humanizer 共有的字句層，不是海明威獨有 |

## 完整範例

### 範例 A：小說段落（英文 specimen，Sepia 撰寫；非海明威文本）

**Before（AI 傾向的寫法）。** 主題被講白、情緒全走身體感受、天氣鏡射心境、結尾補一句意義。

> The letter sat on the kitchen table like an accusation. Mara's chest tightened as she read it a second time, the words blurring as grief and betrayal warred inside her. Outside, the rain had begun again, as if the sky itself understood what she had lost. She realized then that trust, once broken, could never truly be mended, and that the mother she had known had been a stranger all along.

**After（海明威方法：省略、行為與對話、直述句、一段 "and" 並列）。**

> The letter was on the kitchen table where her mother kept the receipts. Mara read it standing up. Then she sat down and read it again.
>
> "You knew," she said.
>
> Nobody answered. It was raining and the window over the sink was open and the rain came in on the sill and she did not close it. She put the kettle on. When it boiled she made the tea and drank it and it was too hot and she drank it anyway.
>
> Teo's was forty minutes on the highway. She put the letter in her coat.

**逐處對應。**

| 改動 | 依據 | 對應 sepia 軸 |
|---|---|---|
| 刪掉「trust, once broken…」與「a stranger all along」 | 冰山：讀者要「feel something more than they understood」；Smith 1983 證明結局意義是他刻意刪的東西 | Thematic explicitness、Narratorial commentary |
| 「chest tightened」「grief and betrayal warred」→ 站著讀、坐下再讀、開窗不關、茶太燙照喝 | 「It is made; not described」；情緒用動作 | Emotion via embodied sensation 81% → 行為主導的人類帶 |
| 天氣不再「understood what she had lost」，雨只是雨，而且進到窗台 | 省略敘事者的解釋 | Setting mirrors inner states 4.07 → 3.58 |
| 「"You knew," she said. Nobody answered.」 | Lamb 2010：對話承擔敘事；Rice：對話量兩倍 | Dialogue as information carrier |
| "It was raining and the window … and the rain … and she did not close it" | Levin 1951 所析的並列與 "and" 串接；A Moveable Feast 的 "simple declarative sentence" | 句長交錯：一段長並列句夾在短句之間，避免節拍器 |
| 「like an accusation」「as if the sky itself」刪除 | 星報：避免 extravagant 修飾；Fable 5.1 廠商指南的 mannered prose 同類 | style-pass cliché／purple prose |
| 保留 "Then" | Rice：他的副詞是 then／now 這類 | style-pass §4 restore plain connectives |

**註記：After 裡的收據、水壺、Teo、四十分鐘是 specimen 作者在 write／recreate 下自行提供的事實，Before 裡沒有。refactor 不得自行生出這些具體物；它只能刪 Before 有的、改寫 Before 有的。**

**這個 After 的已知代價（review 時應報告、不應修）。** 內心通道接近低極端，rubric 會給 over-correction advisory；如果整篇每段都是這個節奏，voice-skills 的均勻性 finding 成立。海明威自己在 "Indian Camp"（1925，美國公有領域）結尾就用了直白命名："he felt quite sure that he would never die."，所以 voice profile 不該禁止命名，只該把它留給少數位置。

### 範例 B：專業文體（事故摘要，星報規則）

**Before。**

> During the course of the incident, which unfortunately began at approximately 14:02 UTC, our team was not able to immediately identify the root cause, but eventually the issue was traced back to a misconfiguration in the retry queue, which caused a significant and highly impactful cascade of failures across multiple downstream services that were not expecting such a magnificent volume of traffic.

**After。**

> At 14:02 UTC the retry queue began resending failed jobs without jitter. Downstream services received 412 retries inside one 200 ms window and rate-limited each other. We found the cause at 14:41 and set `RETRY_JITTER=full` at 14:47.

**逐處對應。**

| 改動 | 星報規則（逐字） | 對應 sepia 檢查 |
|---|---|---|
| 一句 71 字拆成三句 | "Use short sentences." | professional-pass check 10 fluency；style-pass §1 poor sentence structure |
| 「unfortunately」「significant and highly impactful」「magnificent」刪除 | "Avoid the use of adjectives, especially such extravagant ones as splendid, gorgeous, grand, magnificent" | style-pass §3 inflation adjectives |
| 「was not able to immediately identify」→ 直接寫何時找到 | "Be positive, not negative." | postmortems domain：時間戳記、機制優先 |
| 「During the course of」「approximately」「eventually」 | "Eliminate every superfluous word." | professional-pass check 2 density |
| 「, but eventually the issue was traced」的 but | "common abuse of the conjunction"：but 暗示了不存在的對比 | discourse 邏輯連接 |
| 補上 412、200 ms、14:41、14:47 | 精確（"heart disease, not heart failure"） | professional-pass check 5 specificity；never invent：這些數字必須來自事故資料 |

## 對 skill 的結構性啟示（Sepia 推論）

1. 海明威方法是**一種聲音**，不是校準目標。它在數個軸上把文本推向人類帶（主題不講白、行為呈現情緒、對話承載），但至少有一個軸與 sepia 的量測方向相反（否定句），也有把 interior access 推到極端的傾向。這正是 voice-skills.md 定義的「強烈美學」；進 sepia 的正確位置是 voice profile，透過既有的 opt-in 介面載入，不改三個 pass。
2. 它天然分兩半：冰山對小說路線，星報規則對專業路線。一個 profile、兩節，各自掛在對應路由上。
3. 「省略需要知道」是 profile 裡最重要的一句。對人類作者這是技法；對模型這是護欄：不知道的東西不能靠省略掩蓋，sepia 的 never-invent-specifics 在 voice 之上仍然全強度。
4. 節奏規則要寫成「短句為主、長並列句為破」，不能寫成「用短句」。Rice 的資料和 Levin 的分析都說他不是全短句；全短句的 profile 會製造 voice-skills 已記錄的節拍器指紋。
5. Hemingway Editor 類工具不進來。它們是字句層 humanizer；StoryScope 在所研究的編輯條件與樣本中量到字句層改寫幾乎不影響偵測，且未測試純字句層介入。sepia 的差異化正在這裡。
